Rosary Intentions

We pray the Rosary...

  • for the holy souls in purgatory
  • for peace throughout the whole world
  • for the daily conversion of all sinners
  • for our perseverance in our spiritual conversion
  • for us, that you may help us carry our crosses
  • for families, that they may pray together and attain holiness
  • for children, that they may not lose their innocence, and for young people, that they may draw near to God without being ashamed of it
  • for all people throughout the world who suffer physically, mentally, spiritually, materially, and from injustices
  • for all who are dying; most holy Mother, intercede with God so that they may be granted strength
  • for those consecrated to God, priests, and nuns, that they may follow the guidance of the Holy Spirit
  • for the intentions of Pope Leo XIV, that, guided by the Holy Spirit, he may continue to lead the holy Catholic Church along the right path
  • We ask you, Mother, to intercede with God, that by his grace he may raise up more priestly vocations, which we so greatly need: “Ask the master of the harvest to send out laborers for his harvest.” (Matthew 9:38)

We fly to your protection, O Holy Mother of God. Do not despise our petitions in our necessities, but deliver us always from all dangers, O glorious and blessed Virgin; shelter us beneath your sacred and blessed mantle from the attacks and snares of the evil enemy and his followers
You who are the temple of the Holy Spirit, grant that the Holy Spirit may descend upon our home and each one of us with his gifts
Guard and protect us together with your spouse, the patriarch Saint Joseph, terror of demons, protector of the Holy Family, our spiritual father, protector of the holy Church, and the earthly father of your Son
Together with our holy guardian angels,
Together with the holy Archangels Michael, Gabriel, and Raphael, protectors of heaven and earth
Together with all the good angelic powers of God the Father Almighty,
Together with all the saints, our intercessors and examples to follow
But especially, dearest Mother, we ask you to help us have a more humble heart
Forgive our selfishness and, above all, help us strengthen our faith and trust in your beloved Son, our Lord
For all our personal intentions...

We ask this through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Amen
