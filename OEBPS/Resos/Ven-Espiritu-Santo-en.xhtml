Come, Holy Spirit

Come, Holy Spirit,
fill the hearts of thy faithful
and kindle in them
the fire of thy love.

Send forth, O Lord, thy Spirit,
that he may renew the face of the earth.

Let us pray.

O God, who didst fill
the hearts of thy faithful
with the light of the Holy Spirit,
grant that,
guided by the same Spirit,
we may judge aright
and ever rejoice in thy consolation.

Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Amen
