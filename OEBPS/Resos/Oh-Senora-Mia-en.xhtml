O My Lady

O my Lady, O my Mother, I offer myself entirely to you,

and as proof of my filial affection, this day I consecrate to you my eyes, my ears, my tongue, and my heart,

in a word, my whole being, since I am entirely yours,

O Mother of goodness, keep me and protect me as your child.

Amen.
