Prayer to the Holy Spirit

Come, O Holy Spirit, hear us; Spirit of the Father, give us life; Spirit of the Son, save us.

O Eternal Love, fill us; set us aflame with your fire; enlighten us with your light.

Living fountain, satisfy us; wash us from our sins; strengthen us with your anointing.

Refresh us with your consolation; guide us by your grace; protect us by your angels.

May we never be separated from you, O God the Holy Spirit; hear us.

Touch us with the finger of your grace; pour over us a torrent of virtue.

Strengthen us with your gifts, and refresh us with your fruits.

Deliver us from the evil enemy; anoint us in our final struggle; defend us at the hour of death.

Then call us to you, that with all the saints we may praise the Father, the Son, and you, O gracious and eternal Comforter.

Amen
