Ave Maris Stella

Hail, star of the sea,
Mother who brought forth God,
yet ever remaining Virgin,
happy gate of heaven.

Receiving that Ave
from the mouth of Gabriel,
establish us in peace,
changing the name of Eve.

Loose the bonds of the guilty,
give light to the blind,
drive away our ills,
obtain for us every good.

Show that you are a mother:
through you may he receive
our prayers, who, born for us,
deigned to be yours.

Peerless Virgin,
gentle above all others,
make us, set free from guilt,
meek and chaste.

Grant us a pure life,
prepare a safe path,
so that, seeing Jesus,
we may rejoice for ever.

Glory be to God the Father,
praise to Christ most high,
and to the Holy Spirit,
to the three one honor.

Amen.
