Divine Providence

Most Holy Trinity,
O Divine Providence!

Grant me your mercy
and your infinite goodness.

Kneeling at your feet,
O marvel of charity,

I ask you for my loved ones:
a home, clothing, and sustenance.

Grant them health,
lead them along the right path;

may virtue always be
what guides their destiny.

You are all my hope,
you are my consolation;

as far as my mind can comprehend,
in you I believe,
in you I hope,
and in you I trust.

Amen.
