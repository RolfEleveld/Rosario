Litany of the Holy Spirit

Lord, have mercy.

Lord, have mercy.

Christ, have mercy.

Christ, have mercy.

Lord, have mercy.

Lord, have mercy.

Divine Holy Spirit,

hear us.

Holy Spirit, the Paraclete,

graciously hear us.

God the Father of heaven,

have mercy on us.

God the Son, Redeemer of the world,

have mercy on us.

God the Holy Spirit,

have mercy on us.

Most Holy Trinity, who are one God,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of truth,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of wisdom,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of understanding,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of fortitude,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of piety,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of good counsel,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of knowledge,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of holy fear,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of charity,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of joy,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of peace,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of the virtues,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of all grace,

have mercy on us.

Spirit of the adoption of the children of God,

have mercy on us.

Purifier of our souls,

have mercy on us.

Sanctifier and guide of the Catholic Church,

have mercy on us.

Distributor of heavenly gifts,

have mercy on us.

Knower of the thoughts and intentions of the heart,

have mercy on us.

Sweetness of those who begin to serve you,

have mercy on us.

Crown of the perfect,

have mercy on us.

Joy of the angels,

have mercy on us.

Light of the patriarchs,

have mercy on us.

Inspiration of the prophets,

have mercy on us.

Word and wisdom of the apostles,

have mercy on us.

Victory of the martyrs,

have mercy on us.

Knowledge of the confessors,

have mercy on us.

Purity of the virgins,

have mercy on us.

Anointing of all the saints,

have mercy on us.

Be merciful,

spare us, O Lord.

Be merciful,

graciously hear us, O Lord.

From all sin,

deliver us, O Lord.

From all temptations and snares of the devil,

deliver us, O Lord.

From all presumption and despair,

deliver us, O Lord.

From the attack upon known truth,

deliver us, O Lord.

From envy of a brother's grace,

deliver us, O Lord.

From all obstinacy and impenitence,

deliver us, O Lord.

From all negligence and levity of spirit,

deliver us, O Lord.

From all impurity of mind and body,

deliver us, O Lord.

From all heresies and errors,

deliver us, O Lord.

From every evil spirit,

deliver us, O Lord.

From an evil and everlasting death,

deliver us, O Lord.

By your eternal procession from the Father and the Son,

deliver us, O Lord.

By the miraculous conception of the Son of God,

deliver us, O Lord.

By your descent upon Jesus at his baptism,

deliver us, O Lord.

By your holy appearance at the transfiguration of the Lord,

deliver us, O Lord.

By your coming upon the disciples of the Lord,

deliver us, O Lord.

In the day of judgment,

deliver us, O Lord.

We sinners,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you forgive us,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you vouchsafe to give life and holiness to all the members of the Church,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you vouchsafe to grant us the gift of true piety, devotion, and prayer,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you vouchsafe to inspire in us sincere affections of mercy and charity,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you vouchsafe to create in us a new spirit and a pure heart,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you vouchsafe to grant us true peace and tranquility of heart,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you make us worthy and strong to bear persecution for the sake of justice,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you vouchsafe to confirm us in your grace,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you receive us into the number of your elect,

we beseech you, hear us.

That you vouchsafe to hear us,

we beseech you, hear us.

Spirit of God,

we beseech you, hear us.

Lamb of God, who take away the sins of the world,

Send us the Holy Spirit.

Lamb of God, who take away the sins of the world,

Send us the Spirit promised by the Father.

Lamb of God, who take away the sins of the world,

Give us the good Spirit.

Holy Spirit,

hear us.

Spirit of consolation,

graciously hear us.

Send forth your Spirit, and all shall be created.

And you shall renew the face of the earth.

Let us pray:
O God, who didst instruct the hearts of thy faithful
by the light of the Holy Spirit,
grant that, guided by the same Spirit,
we may relish the sweetness of what is good
and ever enjoy his divine consolation,
through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Amen.

Ave Maris Stella
