Prayer to Saint Raphael the Archangel

Glorious Archangel Saint Raphael,
medicine of God,
who didst guide Tobias on his journey
to collect the debt of Gabelus,

didst prepare for him a happy marriage
and restore sight to his aged father:

guide us on the way of salvation,
help us in our needs,
make our homes happy,
and grant us the vision of God in heaven.

Amen.
