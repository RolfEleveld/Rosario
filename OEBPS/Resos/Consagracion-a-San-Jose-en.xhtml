Consecration to Saint Joseph

Msgr. João Scognamiglio Clá Dias

O Most Holy spouse of Mary, before you are those whom you have aided in a special way, in all circumstances, and who take you as the patron of confidence.

You, O Saint Joseph, fulfilled your mission and took it to its final consequences, in the perfection of your virtues.

And are we, too, not called? Is there not something along our paths to be accomplished? Yes, in this tragic moment, which finds humanity in the delirium of a horrible decadence, each one of us has a specific mission, in view of the Reign of your most pure Spouse. With her help, and with your protection, we must reverse the current situation, combat the world and conquer it. We must, therefore, be upright, prudent and faithful.

However, we acknowledge our human insufficiency in face of this grand prospect. We fly to you, asking you to receive us paternally and to accept the consecration that we make to you. We place our souls and all that we have at the feet of Our Lord Jesus Christ, through you. As the head of the Holy Family, your relationship of authority over the Child Jesus endures in eternity, and He will always grant whatever you do request of Him.

Thus, we beseech you, as Patriarch of the Holy Catholic Church, which you never forsake, to take each one of us in your hands and govern us. And, through your intercession before Mary Most Holy, we implore you to obtain for us your faith and your confidence, the serene conviction that the Holy Church will triumph, the courage of the crusaders, the perfection with which you did react to all perplexity, and the splendour of a holiness such as history has never known.

So be it.
